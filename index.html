AI AGENT MODEL

Understanding the PEAS Model

The Smart Home AI agent can be described using Performance, Environment, Actuators and Sensors.

P

Performance

Save electricity, maintain comfort, respond quickly and reduce unnecessary appliance usage.

E

Environment

A smart home containing rooms, people, appliances, sensors and changing environmental conditions.

A

Actuators

Control lights, fans, AC and other smart appliances or send alerts.

S

Sensors

Temperature, motion, light intensity and electricity consumption sensors provide information to the agent.
