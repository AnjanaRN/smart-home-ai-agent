🤖 AI WORKFLOW

How the AI Agent Works

SmartHome AI follows a simple decision-making process to understand the environment and recommend energy-efficient appliance usage.

🌡️ STEP 1 Sense

The system collects sensor information such as temperature, motion, light level, and energy usage.

🧠 STEP 2 Analyze

Gemini AI evaluates the sensor data to understand the current condition of the home.

⚙️ STEP 3 Decide

The AI determines which appliances should remain ON or be switched OFF.

⚡ STEP 4 Optimize

The recommendation aims to reduce unnecessary electricity consumption.

SENSOR INPUTS

What Does the AI Monitor?

These environmental values help the AI understand what is happening inside the smart home.

🌡️ INPUT

Temperature

Helps determine whether cooling through a fan or air conditioner may be required.

👤 INPUT

Motion Detection

Helps identify whether someone is present in the room or whether the room is empty.

💡 INPUT

Light Level

Helps determine whether artificial lighting is actually required.

⚡ INPUT

Energy Consumption

Helps the AI identify situations where electricity consumption may be unnecessarily high.

🔍 DECISION PROCESS

How Does the AI Make a Decision?

The agent considers multiple conditions before generating its recommendation.

EXAMPLE

Imagine the room temperature is 32°C, someone is present, the light level is low, and energy consumption is relatively high.

AI REASONING

Balance comfort with energy efficiency

The AI can recognize that the room is occupied and warm, while also considering whether lighting and high-power appliances are necessary.

👤 OCCUPANCY Present

Appliance decisions can be useful when someone is actually using the room.

🌡️ TEMPERATURE High

Cooling requirements are considered based on the current temperature.

💡 LIGHTING Required

Low light levels can indicate that artificial lighting may be needed.

⚡ ENERGY Monitor

The AI considers whether high-power appliances should be reduced.

ENERGY EFFICIENCY

Tips & Tricks

Simple practices that can help reduce unnecessary energy consumption in a smart home.

💡 TIP 01

Use Natural Light

When sufficient natural light is available, avoid keeping artificial lights switched on unnecessarily.

🚪 TIP 02

Avoid Empty-Room Usage

Turn off lights, fans, and other appliances when the room is not being used.

🌀 TIP 03

Use Cooling Wisely

Use the appropriate cooling appliance for the current temperature and occupancy conditions.

🔌 TIP 04

Watch High-Power Devices

Avoid unnecessary simultaneous use of multiple high-energy appliances.
